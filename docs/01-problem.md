One-page “plain report card” (start here). It uses short words and colour bands. Example for one test:

Blood sugar (fasting): how much sugar is in your blood after not eating.
Green: below 100. Yellow: 100 to 125. Red: 126 or more.
If red: see your doctor within 1 week.

A doctor must check the wording and the numbers.
Medical word swapper. A small list that replaces hard words with simple ones, printed on the report or given with it. For example, “HbA1c” becomes “your average sugar for the last 3 months”, “lipid profile” becomes “fat levels in your blood”, and “BD” becomes “twice a day”.
Three-line summary on every report. Only three lines: What is high or low. What to do this week. When to see the doctor. Short and clear beats complete.
Prescription helper. A sheet that explains the common shorthand on prescriptions (OD, BD, “after food”, “SOS”) and a reminder to ask the doctor or pharmacist about anything unclear. Avoid telling people what to take, since that is where the risk is.
Helper tool for the relative. Like the nephew: he sends a photo, gets a basic-English summary, checks it, and forwards it. This keeps a human in the loop and lowers medical risk.
Follow-up message after 3 days. “Did you see your doctor about your report?” This targets the delay, which was the real failure in xyz’s story.
Full app or bot (last). Only if ideas 1-3 show people want and use it.
